Only the lowest-level allocations count toward the budget total. Parent totals are calculated from their children, so nested money is never counted twice.

Allocations

Add categories, nest sub-allocations, and track each expense.
A category becomes a parent when you add a sub-allocation. Its amount then automatically equals the sum of its children. You can still add a regular allocation with no children.

Allocation settings

When enabled, leftover budget is shared across eligible top-level allocations. If the budget is lowered, eligible allocations are reduced proportionally. Priority allocations are excluded. Moving one allocation's slider preserves that amount and distributes any leftover among the others.
When an allocation is increased beyond the available budget, non-priority allocations are reduced first. Priority allocations are protected where possible.
FineFast
Controls slider step size: Fine gives smaller adjustments; Fast gives larger jumps. Type an exact amount in the amount field.
